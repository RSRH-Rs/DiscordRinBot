邀请小凛时要的权限What Rin asks for when you invite her

点“邀请小凛”后，Discord 会列出下面这 16 项让你勾选。每项都写了小凛拿它做什么。不想给的可以取消勾选，这里会告诉你哪些功能会受影响。After you click Invite, Discord lists these 16 permissions for you to tick. Each one says what Rin uses it for. Untick any you don’t want to give, and this page shows what stops working.

小凛 想在你的服务器里获得这些权限Rin would like these permissions in your server和 Discord 邀请页上的一样，顺序可能不同Same as on Discord’s invite page, the order may differ

邀请之后：把小凛的身份组往上拖After inviting: drag Rin’s role up

Discord 规定，机器人只能给比自己低的身份组发放、只能处罚比自己低的成员。权限都给了，位置不对也会失败。Discord only lets a bot hand out roles below its own and act on members below it. Even with every permission, the wrong position makes it fail.

在 Discord 的「服务器设置 › 身份组」里，把「小凛」拖到要自动发放的身份组上面。In Discord, open Server Settings › Roles and drag “Rin” above the roles she should hand out.

管理员的身份组可以留在小凛上面，这样小凛就管不到他们。Keep your admins’ roles above Rin so she can’t act on them.

  1. 管理员Admin
  2. 小凛Rin放在这里put her here
  3. 新朋友New friend
  4. 成员Member
  5. @everyone

小凛不会要的权限Permissions Rin never asks for

这些权限太大，小凛用不到，邀请链接里也没有。These are too broad for what Rin does, so the invite link leaves them out.

  • 管理员Administrator等于什么都能做，所以不要。It allows everything, so Rin doesn’t take it.
  • 管理服务器Manage Server改服务器名称、图标和设置，小凛不碰这些。Changes the server’s name, icon and settings. Rin leaves those alone.
  • 提及 @everyoneMention @everyone小凛的消息不会 @全体或 @身份组。Rin’s messages never ping everyone or a role.
  • 管理 WebhookManage Webhooks日志直接用小凛自己发，不借用 Webhook。Logs are posted by Rin herself, not through webhooks.
